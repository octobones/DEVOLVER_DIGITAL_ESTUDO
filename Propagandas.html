Shroom and Gloom is Emerging into Early Access on September 10

Our punk partners from Team Lazerbeam have cooked long enough and they are ready to set their first-person double-deck-builder Shroom and Gloom free as an Early Access release on September 10. This unique unique roguelike about exploring the depths of hand-drawn dungeons and using hundreds of cards spread across two distinct decks to unleash red hot synergies and delicious combos on hordes of grotesque fungal foes. You can find out more about it in their latest dev log, which goes in depth on everything that awaits you in the Gloom.

If you can't wait until September 10 to experience Shroom and Gloom’s imaginative take on dungeon-crawling and deck-
building, check out the demo on Steam now for an early taste of the action.

Serious Sam: Shatterverse Launches August 31

Hey folks, Our friends from Behaviour cooked long enough, and now it’s time for Serious Sam: Shatterverse to launch. Coming August 31 to PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, Shatterverse brings classic Serious Sam run-and-gun chaos merged with a roguelite multiverse where apparently one Sam just wasn't enough.  Mental has shattered reality, so you'll team up with alternate Sams, blast through shifting universes, stack ridiculous upgrades, and point a deeply irresponsible arsenal at hordes of enemies. Expect big arenas, bigger guns, and plenty of backpedaling while everything around you blows to pieces.

Can't wait until August 31? The Steam Playtest is open now, so jump in and start shooting holes in the multiverse early.

Dark Scrolls is Out Now

Hey everyone. Drop what you’re doing, because Dark Scrolls is officially out right now on Steam. Our friends at Doinksoft have been locking themselves in a room cooking this one up, and if you’ve played Gato Roboto or Gunbrella, you already know the kind of beautiful madness they like to build. This time around, they wanted to fuse chaotic bullet-hell energy with roguelite progression, wrapped in a pixel-perfect fantasy world that has absolutely zero respect for your free time.

We’re running a launch special for the next two weeks, where you can secure a 10% discount. Even better, we are throwing in free copies of Gunbrella and Gato Roboto just for picking it up early on PC or Switch.
